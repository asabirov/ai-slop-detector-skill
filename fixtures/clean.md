## Quoting what the rules catch

A backtick can appear on its own, in a shell prompt or a stray ` in a sentence,
without shifting every span after it.

The service reaches the graph over `neo4j://graph:7687`. A `<style>` block is
not the whole sheet, and an `app://` URI in `<div>` prose is not a link.
